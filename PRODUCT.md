# PRODUCT.md — Product Context & Purpose

## Platform
- **Type**: Desktop & Tablet Web Application
- **Framework**: React 18 + Vite (Frontend) + FastAPI (Backend AI Orchestrator)

## Target Audience & Persona
- **Primary Users**: Enterprise Customer Support Agents, Contact Center Reps
- **Secondary Users**: Contact Center Operations Managers, QA Quality Auditors
- **Context of Use**: Fast-paced live support chat environment. Agents need to read fast, make split-second decisions, and de-escalate frustrated customers under high SLA pressure.

## Product Positioning
- Real-time AI copilot that predicts CSAT, forecasts churn risk, and suggests 1-click empathetic resolutions without adding cognitive clutter.

## Evidence & Claims
- Verified sub-5ms BM25 in-memory RAG policy retrieval.
- Automated ISO-9001 quality compliance auditing.
